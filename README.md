FOUNDRY is a 5v5 tactical FPS built with three.js: the Embers plant a charge at the Kiln or wipe the Wardens out, the Wardens hold the site, and every round earns money for better weapons. Bots route across the map, hold positions, and react to sight and sound. First to 5 rounds takes the match.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Click to play. WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, R to reload, B for the buy menu, E (hold) to plant at the Kiln, G/H for grenades. Four invented weapons, two grenades, a buy economy, and a fixed-step simulation.
